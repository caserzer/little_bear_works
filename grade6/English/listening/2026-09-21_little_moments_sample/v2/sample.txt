Little Moments.
One More, Please!

It's Saturday morning, and the bakery smells amazing.
Warm bread. Sweet cinnamon. And something buttery.

Dad smiles at me. "You're ordering today, Lucy."

"Me?"

"Yep. I'll be right here."

Okay. I can do this.

The woman behind the counter waves.
"Hi there! What can I get for you?"

"Could I have two cinnamon rolls, please?"

"Sure! For here or to go?"

I look at Dad.
He points to the little paper bags.

"To go, please."

The woman reaches for a bag.
Then I remember my little brother, Ben.
He's at home, probably asking Mom when breakfast is coming.

"Actually, could we make that three?"

"Of course. Three cinnamon rolls."

Dad raises an eyebrow.
"Who's the third one for?"

"Ben!"

"Good thinking. He'd notice if we came home smelling like cinnamon."

I laugh.
Then I spot a tray of tiny cookies shaped like cats.
One has a crooked ear.
It looks a little like our cat, Noodle.

"Dad, look! That one's Noodle."

"It does look like him."

"Can we get a cookie, too?"

Dad checks the bag.
"Let's stick with the rolls today."

"Okay. Maybe next time."

The woman hands me our breakfast.

"How much is it?"

"Nine dollars, please."

Dad pays, and I take the bag.
It's warm against my fingers.

"Could we have a few napkins, please?"

"Absolutely. Here you go."

"Thank you! Have a nice day!"

"You, too!"

Outside, Dad grins.
"You handled that really well."

I grin back. "I only forgot Ben for about three seconds."

At home, Ben runs to the door.
"Did you get me anything?"

I hold up the bag.
"One cinnamon roll. Just for you."

"You're the best!"

Noodle walks over and sniffs the bag.

"Sorry, Noodle," I tell him.
"Your breakfast is in your bowl."

He flicks his tail and walks away.

Dad laughs.
"I think he wants to speak to the manager."
