# 36 · The Not-So-Lost Remote

遥控器没有走远。大家找不到遥控器，决定按照最后使用的线索逐步寻找。

情境表达：回忆经过、描述位置、请求挪动物品。

词语：remote — 遥控器；cushion — 靠垫；underneath — 在下面。

常用句：

- Who had it last?
- Could you move over a little?
- Let's check one place at a time.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Not-So-Lost Remote. We are ready for a nature show. The snacks are ready, the lights are low, and the television is off because the remote has disappeared. Dad checks the coffee table. I check under a magazine. Ben checks his snack bowl. Why would it be in your snack bowl?

**弟弟 Ben**

I like to be thorough.

**爸爸**

Who had it last?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I used it this afternoon. Then I put it on the couch. I think. Maybe beside the blue cushion.

**弟弟 Ben**

Maybe the couch ate it. It eats my pencils all the time.

**爸爸**

Let's check one place at a time. Lucy, look beside the couch. Ben, check under the cushions.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I find a sock, two building blocks, and a pencil. The couch clearly has hobbies. Ben lifts one cushion, then the next. Still no remote.

**弟弟 Ben**

Dad, could you move over a little?

**爸爸**

Why? Do you think it's underneath me?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Dad stands up. There, exactly where he was sitting, is the remote. Nobody speaks for a second. Then Ben slowly raises one eyebrow.

**弟弟 Ben**

We checked almost everywhere except the most suspicious person.

**爸爸**

In my defense, it was surprisingly comfortable.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The television finally turns on. After the show, we put the remote in a small tray on the table. Dad checks his seat before sitting down again. The couch may eat pencils, but tonight we've rescued its biggest snack.
