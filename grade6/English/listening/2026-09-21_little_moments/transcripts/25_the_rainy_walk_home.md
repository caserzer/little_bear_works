# 25 · The Rainy Walk Home

雨天一起回家。放学下雨，两个朋友商量怎样共用一把伞。

情境表达：提出同行、提醒注意、商量路线。

词语：umbrella — 雨伞；puddle — 水坑；shortcut — 近路。

常用句：

- Do you want to share my umbrella?
- Let's take the longer way.
- You're getting wet on that side.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Rainy Walk Home. School ends, and rain is falling hard outside the front doors. Emma opens her umbrella. I open my backpack and find a notebook, a snack, and no umbrella at all.

**朋友 Emma**

Do you want to share my umbrella?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes, please. Mine is doing an excellent job of staying dry at home. We step outside together and immediately discover that walking under one umbrella takes teamwork.

**朋友 Emma**

You're getting wet on that side. Come a little closer.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I move closer. Our backpacks bump. We take three steps and bump again. After a minute, we find a rhythm that doesn't involve walking into each other.

**朋友 Emma**

Should we take the shortcut through the park?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Let's take the longer way. The path gets muddy, and I don't want to bring half the park home on my shoes.

**朋友 Emma**

Good point. Sidewalk it is.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

At the corner, we stop before a giant puddle. I go around the left side while Emma starts going right. The umbrella can't decide who to follow.

**朋友 Emma**

Same side, Lucy. We need to choose the same side.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Right. This is more complicated than our group project. We laugh and carefully walk around together. When we reach my house, Mom opens the door with a towel.

**妈妈**

Come in, both of you. You look like you've had an adventure.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

A very small, very wet adventure. Tomorrow, my umbrella is coming to school. It has enjoyed enough time off.
