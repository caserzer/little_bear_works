# 24 · A Haircut, Just a Little

头发只剪一点点。理发前说清楚想剪多少，比只说一点点更有用。

情境表达：描述长度、澄清程度、确认效果。

词语：haircut — 理发；trim — 修剪；shoulder — 肩膀。

常用句：

- Just a little off, please.
- About this much?
- I'd like to keep it long.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. A Haircut, Just a Little. Mom takes me to the hair salon after school. I sit in a big chair while the stylist puts a cape around my shoulders. In the mirror, I look like a person wearing a very fancy tablecloth.

**理发师**

What would you like today?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Just a little off, please.

**理发师**

Sure. About this much?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

She holds her fingers a short distance apart. Maybe a little less. I'd like to keep it long.

**理发师**

No problem. I'll trim the ends and keep it below your shoulders. Does that sound right?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes, exactly. I feel better now that we've agreed on what a little means. Mom sits nearby, reading a magazine with a very serious cake on the cover.

**妈妈**

Comfortable?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Mostly. My nose has decided to itch, and my hands are trapped under this cape.

**理发师**

You can move your hand out. Just tell me before you move your head.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I scratch my nose, and the haircut continues. Small pieces of hair fall to the floor. When the stylist finishes, she gives me a hand mirror so I can see the back.

**理发师**

What do you think?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I like it. It still feels like my hair, just a little neater.

**妈妈**

Ready to go?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Almost. I turn my head once more in the mirror. A little was exactly the right amount. Next time, I'll use my fingers to show it instead of expecting someone to guess.
