# 18 · The Group Project Problem

小组作业怎么分工。海报作业快到期限，两个朋友重新商量分工。

情境表达：分配任务、说明进度、提出帮助。

词语：project — 项目作业；outline — 提纲；deadline — 截止时间。

常用句：

- What have we finished so far?
- Could you handle the pictures?
- Let's make a simple plan.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Group Project Problem. Emma and I are making a poster about our neighborhood. Yesterday, we both thought the other person was bringing the pictures. Today, we have a beautiful blank sheet of paper.

**朋友 Emma**

What have we finished so far?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The title. And I brought a very nice marker.

**朋友 Emma**

Great. At this speed, we'll finish by the time we're eighty.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Let's make a simple plan. I open my notebook and draw two columns. We need pictures, short descriptions, a map, and a little time to put everything together.

**朋友 Emma**

I can draw the map. I actually like drawing maps. Could you handle the pictures?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Sure. I've already taken photos of the park and the library. I can print them tonight. We each choose two places to write about, then agree to bring everything tomorrow.

**老师**

How's the project going over here?

**朋友 Emma**

Much better now. We finally gave the jobs to actual people instead of hoping they would do themselves.

**老师**

That's usually helpful. Leave time to check the whole poster before you hand it in.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The next day, Emma brings the map, and I bring the pictures. We lay everything out before we start gluing. One picture is upside down, and one street has an extra letter. We fix both.

**朋友 Emma**

Look at that. A real poster.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

And we're nowhere near eighty. I put a check beside every job on our list. Even the nice marker finally gets something useful to do.
