# 20 · Please Don't Feed the Ducks

给鸭子当观众。姐弟俩在公园发现标牌，换一种方式亲近鸭子。

情境表达：读标牌、提醒规则、提出替代活动。

词语：sign — 标牌；pond — 池塘；watch — 观察。

常用句：

- What does that sign say?
- Let's watch them from here.
- That one looks like it's in a hurry.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. Please Don't Feed the Ducks. Ben and I walk around the park pond with Dad. Ben is carrying the last piece of his sandwich. When three ducks swim toward us, he starts pulling the bread into little pieces.

**爸爸**

Hold on a second. What does that sign say?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Please don't feed the ducks. Ben looks at the bread, then at the ducks. They seem extremely interested in ignoring the sign.

**弟弟 Ben**

But they came all this way. It feels rude not to offer them anything.

**爸爸**

The park asks visitors to let the ducks find their own food. We can enjoy watching them without feeding them.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Let's watch them from here. We sit on a bench a little way back from the water. Ben keeps his sandwich for himself, and I start counting ducks.

**弟弟 Ben**

That one looks like it's in a hurry.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

A small duck paddles past a much bigger one and disappears behind some reeds. Maybe it's late for duck school.

**爸爸**

Or maybe it just knows a good place to look for lunch.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We see one duck shake water off its feathers and another tuck its head down for a rest. Suddenly, watching them feels more interesting than throwing bread.

**弟弟 Ben**

The sleepy one is me on Monday morning.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

On the way out, we put our sandwich wrapper in a trash can. Ben waves at the pond. The ducks don't wave back, but one gives a very loud quack. We decide that counts.
