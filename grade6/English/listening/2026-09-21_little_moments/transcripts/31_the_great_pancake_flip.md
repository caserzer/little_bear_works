# 31 · The Great Pancake Flip

煎饼翻面挑战。周末早餐，Lucy学着耐心等煎饼做好再翻面。

情境表达：按顺序操作、请求示范、分工合作。

词语：pancake — 煎饼；spatula — 锅铲；batter — 面糊。

常用句：

- Can you show me how?
- Is it ready to flip?
- Let's try a smaller one.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Great Pancake Flip. Sunday breakfast smells wonderful. Mom is making pancakes, and I've decided I'm old enough to help with the pan. Ben has decided he's old enough to help with the eating. Very different jobs. Can you show me how?

**妈妈**

Sure. Stand beside me, and keep the pan handle turned inward. I'll help you with the hot pan. You can start by pouring a small circle of batter.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I pour a circle that looks more like a sleepy cloud. We watch little bubbles appear on top. Is it ready to flip?

**妈妈**

Almost. Wait until the edges look set. Then slide the spatula underneath.

**弟弟 Ben**

I believe in you, pancake cloud.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I try to turn it, but one edge folds over. It lands looking like a blanket that someone forgot to straighten.

**妈妈**

That's okay. Let's try a smaller one. The first pancake can be a practice pancake.

**弟弟 Ben**

I volunteer to test the practice pancake.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

This time, I pour less batter and wait a little longer. Mom steadies the pan while I slide the spatula underneath. One careful turn, and the pancake lands flat.

**弟弟 Ben**

Beautiful. A round breakfast miracle.

**妈妈**

Nicely done. I'll finish cooking these while you two set the table.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We bring plates, forks, and fruit to the table. Ben gets the folded pancake, just as he requested. He takes a bite and announces that it tastes exactly like a successful pancake. It simply has a more interesting shape.
