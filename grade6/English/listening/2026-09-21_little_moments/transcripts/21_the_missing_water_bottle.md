# 21 · The Missing Water Bottle

水杯失物招领。Lucy在失物招领处用颜色和细节找回水杯。

情境表达：描述失物、提供特征、确认归属。

词语：lost and found — 失物招领处；sticker — 贴纸；lid — 盖子。

常用句：

- I think I left it in the gym.
- What does it look like?
- That's the one!

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Missing Water Bottle. After gym class, I reach into the side pocket of my backpack. No water bottle. I check the other side, even though it has never lived there. Still nothing.

**朋友 Emma**

When did you last have it?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Before basketball. I think I left it in the gym. We go back, but the bench is empty. Emma suggests checking the school office, where there's a box for lost things.

**学校办公室老师**

Looking for something?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

My water bottle. I had it this morning, and now it's missing.

**学校办公室老师**

What does it look like?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

It's dark blue with a silver lid. There's a little moon sticker on one side and a scratch near the bottom.

**学校办公室老师**

Let's see. We have two blue bottles here. Does either of these look familiar?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The first one has a red lid. The second has my little moon, slightly peeling at one corner. That's the one!

**朋友 Emma**

Your moon brought you home.

**学校办公室老师**

Is your name on it anywhere?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Not yet. I should probably fix that.

**学校办公室老师**

A name label would make it easier to return next time.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

At home, I dry the bottle and add a small label under the moon. Ben offers to write an exciting name instead of Lucy. Later, I tell Emma about it on the phone.

**朋友 Emma**

Like what?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Captain Splash. I decide Lucy is more useful for the school office. Captain Splash can be its name at home, where nobody needs to fill out a lost-and-found form.
