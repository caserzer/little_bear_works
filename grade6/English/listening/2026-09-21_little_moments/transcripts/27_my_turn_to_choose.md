# 27 · My Turn to Choose

这次轮到我选。两个朋友喜欢不同的活动，决定给彼此的选择都留时间。

情境表达：表达偏好、协商顺序、遵守约定。

词语：choose — 选择；turn — 轮次；compromise — 折中办法。

常用句：

- What are you in the mood for?
- How about we do both?
- It's your turn to choose.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. My Turn to Choose. Emma comes over on Saturday afternoon. I have a board game ready on the table. She has brought a ball. We look at each other's plans without saying anything for a moment.

**朋友 Emma**

What are you in the mood for?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

This game. I've been waiting all week to play it.

**朋友 Emma**

I was hoping we could go outside. It's finally stopped raining.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Neither of us wants to sound difficult, so we both say whatever you want. That doesn't help at all. Then Emma looks at the clock.

**朋友 Emma**

How about we do both? Half an hour outside, then we come in for the game.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

That works. We tell Mom where we're going and walk to the little court near my house. Emma shows me a passing game that doesn't involve keeping score. Soon, I'm having too much fun to watch the time.

**朋友 Emma**

Half an hour's up. Ready to head back?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Already? Okay. A deal is a deal. Back at home, we get water and sit down at the table. I explain the rules, and Emma asks a few questions before we start.

**朋友 Emma**

It's your turn to choose. Which piece do you want?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The blue one. Always the blue one. Emma takes the green piece, and we begin. By the end of the afternoon, we've both tried something the other person wanted. We still disagree about which activity was better. Fortunately, nobody needs to win that argument.
