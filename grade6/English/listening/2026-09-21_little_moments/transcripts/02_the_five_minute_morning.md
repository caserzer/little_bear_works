# 02 · The Five-Minute Morning

只剩五分钟的早晨。快迟到了，Lucy却找不到自己的鞋；一家人分头寻找。

情境表达：催促、寻找物品、确认时间。

词语：ready — 准备好的；by the door — 在门边；matching — 成对的。

常用句：

- Has anyone seen my other shoe?
- We've got five minutes.
- I'm ready now.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Five-Minute Morning. My alarm rings, and I turn it off. I close my eyes for just a second. When I open them again, Mom is standing in my doorway with my backpack.

**妈妈**

Good morning, sleepyhead. We've got five minutes. Your breakfast is on the table.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Five minutes? That can't be right. I jump out of bed, pull on my clothes, and discover a problem. Has anyone seen my other shoe?

**弟弟 Ben**

Which one? The left one or the one you're standing on?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I look down. One foot is already wearing a sneaker. Very funny. I need the other one. It's white, and it usually lives next to this one.

**妈妈**

Check by the door. You took them off there yesterday.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I find Dad's boots, Ben's sandals, and one tennis ball. No white sneaker. Then I hear a small voice from the living room.

**弟弟 Ben**

Found it! My stuffed bear was using it as a boat.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Of course he was. I put it on and race to the kitchen. Mom hands me a banana and a sandwich wrapped in paper.

**妈妈**

Breakfast to go today. Tomorrow, let's put everything by the door before bed.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I'm ready now. Wait. Where's my backpack?

**弟弟 Ben**

On your back, Captain.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I reach behind me. He's right again. We make it outside with matching shoes, breakfast, and one whole minute to spare. Tonight, I'm checking my bag before I go to sleep. I'm also hiding my shoes from that bear.
