# 04 · Is This Seat Taken?

这里有人坐吗。Lucy在图书馆找座位，和Emma一起寻找一本有趣的书。

情境表达：询问空位、借书、表达阅读偏好。

词语：seat — 座位；due — 到期的；shelf — 书架。

常用句：

- Is this seat taken?
- I'm looking for something funny.
- When is it due?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. Is This Seat Taken? The school library is busy after lunch. I carry two heavy books between the tables until I spot Emma beside an empty chair. Is this seat taken?

**朋友 Emma**

It's yours. Just watch out for my backpack. It's trying to take over the floor.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I sit down and put my books on the table. One is about mountain climbing. The other is about a detective who loses his own keys. I'm looking for something funny.

**朋友 Emma**

Then I'd start with the detective. Losing your keys while solving a mystery sounds pretty good.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I open the first page. The detective is locked outside his office. Emma leans over, and we both start laughing. We quickly cover our mouths because everyone else is reading quietly.

**图书管理员**

Sounds like you found a good one. Would you like to check it out?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes, please. When is it due?

**图书管理员**

In two weeks. The date is on this slip. You can renew it if nobody else is waiting for it.

**朋友 Emma**

Can I borrow it after you? No spoilers, though.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Deal. I put the library slip inside the book and check my pockets before we leave.

**朋友 Emma**

What are you looking for?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

My house key. I don't want to end up like this detective.

**图书管理员**

A very sensible decision.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

My key is exactly where it belongs. The detective would be impressed. Now he just needs to find his.
