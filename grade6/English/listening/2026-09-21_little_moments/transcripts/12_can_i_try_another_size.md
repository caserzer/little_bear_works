# 12 · Can I Try Another Size?

能换个尺码试试吗。试鞋时Lucy发现好看不等于合脚。

情境表达：询问尺码、试穿、描述舒适度。

词语：size — 尺码；tight — 紧的；heel — 脚跟。

常用句：

- Can I try another size?
- These feel a little tight.
- Take a few steps.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. Can I Try Another Size? Dad takes me shopping for new sneakers because my old ones are getting tight. At the store, I find a purple pair with bright yellow laces. They're exactly the colors I wanted.

**店员**

What size do you usually wear?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

A six, I think. The clerk brings a box, and I sit down to try them on. They look amazing. My toes do not seem as excited as I am.

**爸爸**

How do they feel?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

These feel a little tight. Maybe they'll be okay if I don't move much.

**爸爸**

That could be a problem for shoes.

**店员**

We can try half a size up. You should be able to walk comfortably.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Can I try another size? The clerk brings the bigger pair, and I put both shoes on this time.

**店员**

Take a few steps. See if your heel stays in place and your toes have room.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I walk toward the mirror and back. Much better. I can actually imagine running for the bus in these.

**爸爸**

Hopefully, we'll leave early enough that you won't need to.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

At the counter, the clerk asks if I want to wear them out of the store. I say yes and put my old shoes in the box.

**爸爸**

Ready?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

More than ready. I take one big, comfortable step. The yellow laces bounce. My toes and I have finally agreed on something.
