# 15 · Who Turned Off the Wi-Fi?

家里的网络掉线了。视频突然停住，一家人一起找出简单原因。

情境表达：描述问题、确认连接、请求等待。

词语：frozen — 画面卡住的；plug — 插头；connection — 连接。

常用句：

- The screen is frozen.
- Is it working for you?
- Give it a minute.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. Who Turned Off the Wi-Fi? Emma and I are on a video call, planning our class poster. She holds up a sketch, opens her mouth, and turns into a statue. The screen is frozen. Emma? Can you hear me?

**弟弟 Ben**

If she's doing a robot impression, it's really good.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

My phone says there's no connection. Is it working for you? Ben checks his tablet and shakes his head. We call Dad, who is putting away the vacuum cleaner.

**爸爸**

Let me have a look. Did it stop just now?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

About a minute ago. Dad follows the cable from the router to the wall. The plug is hanging loose beside the outlet.

**爸爸**

That would explain it. I must have unplugged it when I moved the vacuum. My mistake.

**弟弟 Ben**

So the internet wasn't broken. It was just taking a nap.

**爸爸**

Something like that. I'll plug it back in. Give it a minute.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We wait while the little lights come back on. I open our call again, and Emma finally starts moving.

**朋友 Emma**

There you are! Did you see my drawing?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Only the first half. You looked very serious for a while.

**朋友 Emma**

That's my thinking face.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We finish planning on paper, then take photos to share. Dad promises to check the plugs next time he cleans. Ben promises to tell everyone he personally watched the internet wake up. Somehow, that becomes the biggest news of the afternoon.
