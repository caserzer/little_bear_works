# 11 · The Red Sock Mystery

红袜子去了哪里。整理衣服时总有袜子失踪，Lucy和Ben展开调查。

情境表达：配对、描述位置、分配家务。

词语：laundry — 洗好的衣物；pair — 一双；inside out — 里外翻面的。

常用句：

- These two don't match.
- Turn it inside out.
- Can you help me fold these, Ben?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Red Sock Mystery. Saturday morning, Mom drops a basket of clean laundry on the couch. It's warm, fluffy, and much taller than it looked in the dryer. Can you help me fold these, Ben?

**弟弟 Ben**

Only if I can be in charge of socks. I'm very good at socks.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Five minutes later, he hands me a red sock and a striped sock. These two don't match.

**弟弟 Ben**

They both fit on feet. That's something.

**妈妈**

There should be another red one somewhere. Check the bigger clothes before we blame the washing machine.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We shake out a towel. Nothing. We check a pillowcase. Still nothing. Ben holds up Dad's sweatshirt and makes it dance.

**弟弟 Ben**

Maybe the sock moved away. Maybe it wanted a bigger closet.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Wait. There's a little lump in that sleeve. Turn it inside out.

**弟弟 Ben**

Aha! One red sock, hiding from its responsibilities.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We put the pair together and finish the basket. I fold shirts while Ben matches the rest of the socks. This time, he checks the colors and patterns.

**妈妈**

Nice work. You two make a good team.

**弟弟 Ben**

Do good teams get a snack break?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Mom points toward the kitchen. Ben races off, then comes back holding one striped sock.

**弟弟 Ben**

Small problem. This one is alone now.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I look at the empty basket. The mystery is not over. Our snack break, however, is absolutely still happening.
