# 10 · The Almost-Perfect Picnic

差一点完美的野餐。野餐快开始时才发现没带餐具，朋友们一起想办法。

情境表达：检查准备、说明遗漏、调整计划。

词语：picnic — 野餐；fork — 叉子；spread out — 铺开。

常用句：

- Did anyone bring forks?
- I thought you had them.
- We can still make this work.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Almost-Perfect Picnic. Emma and I spread out a blanket in the park. Mom sets down the cooler, and Ben arrives carrying a bag of chips like it's a precious treasure. The weather is perfect. The food looks great. Then I open the pasta salad.

**朋友 Emma**

Did anyone bring forks?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I thought you had them.

**朋友 Emma**

I brought plates. You said you were bringing the other stuff.

**弟弟 Ben**

I brought chips. I did my job beautifully.

**妈妈**

Let's see what we have before we go anywhere. Sandwiches, fruit, crackers, and that pasta salad.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We can still make this work. We eat the sandwiches first. Emma passes around the fruit, and Ben opens his very important bag of chips. Nobody seems unhappy.

**朋友 Emma**

The pasta can come home with us. It'll be an excellent second picnic on the kitchen table.

**妈妈**

Good plan. I'll keep it cold in the cooler until we get home.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We watch a tiny dog trying to carry a stick twice its size. Ben puts a cracker next to his sandwich and says he's building a food castle.

**朋友 Emma**

Next time, let's make one list and put our names beside the things we're bringing.

**弟弟 Ben**

Put me down for chips again. I'm experienced now.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

On the way home, I add forks to our next picnic list. Then I add a second line underneath: Check that someone actually packed the forks. That feels like an important detail.
