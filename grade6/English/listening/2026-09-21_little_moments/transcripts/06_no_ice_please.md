# 06 · No Ice, Please

饮料里不要冰。一次饮品点单里，Lucy练习清楚说明自己的选择。

情境表达：点饮料、确认大小、礼貌纠正。

词语：lemonade — 柠檬水；regular — 普通大小的；receipt — 小票。

常用句：

- Could I get a small lemonade?
- No ice, please.
- Sorry, I asked for no ice.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. No Ice, Please. Emma and I stop at a little sandwich shop after school. It's warm outside, and we both want something to drink. The menu has about twenty choices. My brain wants all twenty.

**朋友 Emma**

I'm getting water. You can study the menu, but I'm not waiting until tomorrow.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Okay, decision made. Could I get a small lemonade?

**店员**

Sure. Regular or strawberry?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Regular, please. And no ice, please.

**店员**

One small regular lemonade with no ice. Anything else?

**朋友 Emma**

A cup of water for me, please.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We move to the end of the counter. A few minutes later, the clerk puts two cups in front of us. My lemonade makes a loud, icy rattle. I glance at Emma.

**朋友 Emma**

You can just tell her. She probably has about ten orders in her head.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Sorry, I asked for no ice.

**店员**

Oh, you're right. Thanks for letting me know. I'll make you another one.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thank you. I was worried that saying something would feel awkward, but the clerk is already smiling at the next customer. Soon, my new drink is ready.

**朋友 Emma**

Better?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Much better. We sit by the window, and I take a sip. Now I only have one problem.

**朋友 Emma**

What is it?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I still want to know what strawberry lemonade tastes like.

**朋友 Emma**

That sounds like a problem for another afternoon.
