# 39 · The Neighborhood Cleanup

社区清洁小队。参加社区活动时，Lucy和朋友分工捡拾垃圾，也懂得请大人处理不认识的物品。

情境表达：报名参与、询问分工、说明发现。

词语：gloves — 手套；cleanup — 清洁活动；team — 小队。

常用句：

- Where should we start?
- I'll hold the bag.
- Let's ask an adult about that.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Neighborhood Cleanup. On Saturday, Dad takes Emma, Ben, and me to a cleanup at the park. We join the volunteers near the entrance and get gloves, bags, and litter pickers. Ben immediately starts opening and closing his picker like a giant bird beak.

**爸爸**

Remember, pick up ordinary litter only. Leave sharp or unfamiliar things alone and show an adult.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Where should we start?

**爸爸**

Along this path. Stay where I can see you, and don't reach into the bushes.

**朋友 Emma**

I'll hold the bag. Lucy, you can use the picker. We'll switch in a few minutes.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We find paper cups, snack wrappers, and a very lonely plastic spoon. Ben spots something shiny near the edge of the path but stops before touching it.

**弟弟 Ben**

I can't tell what that is.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Let's ask an adult about that. Dad calls the organizer over, and we leave it for them to handle. Then we return to the easy things on our stretch of path.

**朋友 Emma**

This looks better already. You can actually see the grass beside the bench.

**弟弟 Ben**

I found another spoon. Maybe they're friends.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

After our turn, we hand in the equipment and wash our hands before having a snack. The park isn't magically perfect, but our little section looks much tidier.

**爸爸**

Nice work, team.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Ben gives his empty hands one last imaginary bird-beak snap. Emma and I laugh. It turns out a cleanup can be useful and slightly ridiculous at the same time.
