# 38 · The Calendar Collision

周六的安排撞车了。两个活动安排在同一时间，Lucy主动联系朋友调整计划。

情境表达：发现时间冲突、改约、确认新时间。

词语：calendar — 日历；reschedule — 改约；available — 有空的。

常用句：

- I think I double-booked myself.
- Would Sunday work instead?
- Let me check my calendar.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Calendar Collision. I tell Mom I'm meeting Emma at the park on Saturday morning. Mom looks up from the family calendar with a puzzled expression. That is never a good sign.

**妈妈**

Saturday morning? We're helping your aunt move some boxes, remember?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Oh. I remembered the boxes, but apparently I forgot what day they belonged to. I think I double-booked myself.

**妈妈**

It happens. Tell Emma as soon as you can, and see whether another time works.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I call Emma and explain the problem. I'm sorry. I should have checked before making plans. Would Sunday work instead?

**朋友 Emma**

Let me check my calendar. Sunday morning is free, but I have a family lunch at twelve.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

How about nine thirty? We could stay for an hour, and you'd have time to get home.

**朋友 Emma**

That works. Same park, by the big slide?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes. I'll put it in my calendar right now. I write down the day, time, and meeting place, then tell Mom about the new plan.

**妈妈**

Good. Now we can all see what's happening.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

On Saturday, we help carry light boxes to my aunt's new apartment. On Sunday, I meet Emma exactly where we agreed. She waves from beside the slide.

**朋友 Emma**

No surprise boxes today?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

None. I checked. We walk around the park and talk about our weekend. My calendar has two clear plans instead of one impossible morning. It isn't exciting to look at, but it works beautifully.
