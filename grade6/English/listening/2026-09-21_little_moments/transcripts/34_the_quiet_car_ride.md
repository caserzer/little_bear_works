# 34 · The Quiet Car Ride

车里安静五分钟。一家人坐车出游，Ben的提问不断，大家商量轮流选音乐。

情境表达：询问剩余时间、轮流选择、提出请求。

词语：playlist — 播放列表；rest stop — 休息站；take turns — 轮流。

常用句：

- How much longer?
- Could we turn it down a little?
- Let's take turns choosing songs.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Quiet Car Ride. We've been in the car for twenty minutes when Ben asks the question Dad knew was coming.

**弟弟 Ben**

How much longer?

**爸爸**

About an hour. We'll stop for a break on the way.

**弟弟 Ben**

And how long until the break?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I look out the window and try not to laugh. Mom opens a playlist on her phone. Music fills the car, which gives Ben something new to talk about.

**弟弟 Ben**

I don't know this song. Can we play mine?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Your favorite song is forty seconds long, and we heard it eight times yesterday.

**妈妈**

Let's take turns choosing songs. One each, then we start again. I'll handle the phone while Dad drives.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Ben chooses first. His song has a lot of clapping. I choose a quieter song next, and Dad taps one finger on the steering wheel. Could we turn it down a little? I'd like to hear the words without the windows shaking.

**妈妈**

Of course. How's that?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Much better. For a few minutes, nobody says anything. The fields slide past, the music plays softly, and even Ben looks peaceful.

**弟弟 Ben**

I have a question.

**爸爸**

Let me guess. How much longer?

**弟弟 Ben**

No. Can my next turn be two songs? Mine are very short.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We all laugh. At the rest stop, we stretch our legs and get some water. Back in the car, I add one extra quiet song to the list. Hope is a beautiful thing.
