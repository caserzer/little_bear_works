# 13 · You Got My Name Almost Right

名字差一点就对了。取餐单把Lucy写成了Lucky，引出轻松的小对话。

情境表达：报名字、拼写、确认订单。

词语：spell — 拼写；order — 订单；pickup — 取餐。

常用句：

- Could you spell that for me?
- I think that's my order.
- Close enough.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. You Got My Name Almost Right. Emma and I order sandwiches at a busy cafe. The person at the register asks for my name, but the coffee machine starts making a loud noise just as I answer.

**店员**

Sorry, what was that?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Lucy.

**店员**

Got it. Two sandwiches. We'll call you when they're ready.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We find a table near the pickup counter. Emma takes off her jacket, and I watch people walk past the window. A minute later, someone calls out a name I don't recognize.

**店员**

Lucky! Order for Lucky!

**朋友 Emma**

Are we lucky today? Because those look a lot like our sandwiches.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I walk over and check the bag. One cheese sandwich, one turkey sandwich. I think that's my order.

**店员**

Lucky?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Lucy, actually. Close enough.

**店员**

Oh, sorry about that. Could you spell that for me?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

L, U, C, Y. The clerk corrects the name on the receipt and gives me the bag. I thank her and bring lunch to our table.

**朋友 Emma**

Congratulations on your new name. Does it come with extra cookies?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Apparently not. I check the bag just in case. Only sandwiches and napkins. But my sandwich has an especially big slice of cheese, so maybe I am lucky.

**朋友 Emma**

Next time, I'll order under my name.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Good idea. Let's see if you become Emily, Emma, or Excellent. I think Excellent would suit you for at least one lunch.
