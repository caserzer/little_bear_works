# 26 · The Package Next Door

送到隔壁的包裹。包裹显示送达却找不到，Lucy和爸爸查看照片后解开谜团。

情境表达：核对地址、说明误送、礼貌求助。

词语：package — 包裹；address — 地址；doorstep — 门前台阶。

常用句：

- The photo doesn't look like our door.
- Could you check the address?
- Thanks for bringing it over.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Package Next Door. Dad ordered a new desk lamp, and the delivery message says it has arrived. I open the front door. There's a welcome mat, a flowerpot, and no lamp. I look behind the flowerpot, even though it's much too small.

**爸爸**

Does the message include a photo?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes, but the photo doesn't look like our door. Our mat is green. The one in the picture says Hello in big yellow letters.

**爸爸**

That's next door. Let's check the house number on the label before we go over.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Dad calls our neighbor, Mrs. Lee. She has just found the package outside and noticed the address. A minute later, she meets us by the gate.

**邻居 Mrs. Lee**

I was about to bring this over. Could you check the address? I think it's yours.

**爸爸**

Yes, that's our number. Thanks for bringing it over.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We carry the box inside. Dad opens it, and I help remove the paper around the lamp. Everything looks fine. We place it on my desk and turn it on.

**爸爸**

Better light for your homework.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Definitely. My potato-shaped rock now looks very important under it.

**爸爸**

Glad the lamp is serving a useful purpose.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We flatten the cardboard box for recycling. I look at the delivery photo one more time. Next time something goes missing, I'll check the details before searching behind a tiny flowerpot. Detective Lucy is learning. Slowly, but with excellent lighting.
