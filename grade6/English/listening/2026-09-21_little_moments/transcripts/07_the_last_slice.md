# 07 · The Last Slice

最后一块披萨。一块披萨引发争论，姐弟俩决定公平分配。

情境表达：表达想要、轮流、商量分配。

词语：slice — 一块披萨；fair — 公平的；split — 分开。

常用句：

- Are you going to eat that?
- Let's split it.
- You cut, and I'll choose.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Last Slice. Movie night is about to start. There are blankets on the couch, drinks on the table, and exactly one slice of pizza left in the box. Ben and I notice it at the same time.

**弟弟 Ben**

Are you going to eat that?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I was thinking about it. Were you?

**弟弟 Ben**

I've been thinking about it for a very long time. Since the last slice I ate.

**爸爸**

Sounds serious. How are you two going to solve this?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We could race for it. Dad raises one eyebrow. Okay, maybe not. Let's split it.

**弟弟 Ben**

But one side has more cheese. And one side has the big mushroom.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

You don't even like mushrooms.

**弟弟 Ben**

I might start tonight.

**爸爸**

Here's an idea. One person cuts, and the other person chooses a piece.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

You cut, and I'll choose. Ben studies the pizza like it's a very important map. Dad helps him use the pizza cutter, and we look at the two pieces.

**弟弟 Ben**

They're almost the same. Except this one has a tiny extra corner.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I choose the other piece. Ben looks surprised, then takes the corner with a smile. We sit down just as the movie starts.

**爸爸**

Everybody happy?

**弟弟 Ben**

Very happy. Next time, though, could we order one more pizza?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Dad laughs. I take a bite and discover the big mushroom on my piece. Apparently, Ben has decided not to start liking mushrooms tonight after all.
