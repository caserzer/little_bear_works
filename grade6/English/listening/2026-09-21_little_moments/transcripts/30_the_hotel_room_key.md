# 30 · The Hotel Room Key

打不开房门的房卡。旅行入住后发现房卡没有反应，一家人回前台求助。

情境表达：描述故障、请求帮助、核对房间。

词语：key card — 房卡；front desk — 前台；elevator — 电梯。

常用句：

- Our key card isn't working.
- Could you help us, please?
- Try it one more time.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Hotel Room Key. We're spending the weekend in another town. After checking in, Dad, Ben, and I take the elevator to the third floor. Ben insists on carrying the room key because it makes him feel official.

**弟弟 Ben**

Welcome to our extremely fancy vacation. Please follow me.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

He taps the card against the door lock. A red light flashes. He tries again, more dramatically this time. Still red.

**爸爸**

Let's check the room number first.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The number is right. Dad tries the card, too, but the door stays closed. We take the elevator back to the lobby. Ben looks less official now.

**爸爸**

Our key card isn't working. Could you help us, please?

**酒店前台**

Of course. May I confirm the room number and the name on the reservation?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Dad gives her the details. She checks the booking and makes us a replacement card.

**酒店前台**

Here you go. Try it one more time. If there's still a problem, we'll send someone up to help.

**弟弟 Ben**

Does the card know we're on vacation now?

**酒店前台**

It should be fully informed.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Upstairs, Dad taps the new card. The light turns green, and the door opens. Ben marches in and immediately claims the bed nearest the window.

**爸爸**

Hold on. We haven't decided that yet.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Ben sits down with a sigh. Being the key-card manager, apparently, doesn't include choosing everybody's beds. Our vacation is off to a very normal family start.
