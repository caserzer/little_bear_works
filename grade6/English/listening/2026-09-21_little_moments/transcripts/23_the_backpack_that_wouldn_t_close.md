# 23 · The Backpack That Wouldn't Close

拉不上拉链的书包。Lucy带了太多东西，和爸爸一起整理书包。

情境表达：确认需要、取出多余物品、整理空间。

词语：zipper — 拉链；pocket — 口袋；leave behind — 留下不带。

常用句：

- Do you need all of that?
- Let's take everything out.
- That can stay at home.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Backpack That Wouldn't Close. It's Sunday evening, and my backpack refuses to close. I pull the zipper, push on the top, and try asking nicely. Nothing works. Dad walks past my door and stops.

**爸爸**

Do you need all of that?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Probably. Maybe. I'm prepared for everything.

**爸爸**

Let's take everything out. Then we can see what everything actually means.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We put my things on the bed. Three notebooks, two library books, a pencil case, a sweater, an old art project, and a rock that looks a little like a potato.

**爸爸**

Is the rock part of your homework?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

No. I found it last week. It has a nice personality.

**爸爸**

Then it can keep your desk company. That can stay at home.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I check tomorrow's schedule. I only need two notebooks, and the art project was finished days ago. One library book is due tomorrow, so that goes back in.

**弟弟 Ben**

Can I have the potato rock?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

You may visit it on my desk. It's not ready to move out. I put the heavier books close to the back and the smaller things in the front pocket. The zipper finally closes without a fight.

**爸爸**

How does it feel now?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Much better. I lift the backpack with one hand, then try it on. Ben watches carefully from the doorway.

**弟弟 Ben**

Your bag looks happier.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

So do my shoulders. The rock remains on my desk, looking quietly supportive. Tomorrow, I will be prepared for school, which is probably enough.
