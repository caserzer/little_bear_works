# 17 · The Tiny Birthday Secret

小小的生日秘密。Lucy和Emma准备惊喜卡片，Ben努力保守秘密。

情境表达：邀请帮忙、约定时间、保守惊喜。

词语：surprise — 惊喜；card — 卡片；secret — 秘密。

常用句：

- Don't tell her yet.
- What time should I come over?
- I'll keep it a secret.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Tiny Birthday Secret. Mom's birthday is tomorrow. Emma has offered to help me make a big card, and Ben has offered to help by asking a lot of questions. I call Emma after dinner.

**朋友 Emma**

What time should I come over?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Ten tomorrow morning. Dad's taking Mom out for breakfast. We'll have about an hour to finish the card and set the table.

**弟弟 Ben**

Are we having cake? Is the cake a secret? Can I taste the secret?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Everything is a secret until Mom gets home. Don't tell her yet.

**弟弟 Ben**

I'll keep it a secret. I am excellent at not talking.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

He proves this by talking for another five minutes. The next morning, Emma arrives with colored paper and stickers. We write little messages inside the card. Mine says thank you for helping me even when I'm grumpy.

**朋友 Emma**

That's a good message. Mine says happy birthday to the best pancake maker I know.

**弟弟 Ben**

Mine says I love you more than cake. Is that believable?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Very generous of you. We hear the front door, so we hide the card behind our backs. Mom walks into the kitchen and stops.

**妈妈**

Why is everybody standing so strangely?

**弟弟 Ben**

No reason. Definitely not because of a giant card.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We all laugh and hold it up. Surprise! Mom reads every message slowly. Then she hugs us, including our terrible little secret keeper. The card is slightly crooked, but her smile is perfect.
