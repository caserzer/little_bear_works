# 19 · A Table by the Window

想坐窗边的位置。餐厅没有空窗边桌，Lucy学会在等待和先入座之间选择。

情境表达：询问等位、表达偏好、确认人数。

词语：table — 餐桌；available — 可用的；wait — 等待。

常用句：

- A table for three, please.
- How long is the wait?
- This table is fine.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. A Table by the Window. Mom, Ben, and I stop at a diner for lunch. I spot a table by the window with sunlight falling across it. Unfortunately, another family spots it about three seconds before I do.

**妈妈**

A table for three, please.

**店员**

Of course. We have a booth available near the back.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Could we sit by the window instead?

**店员**

You can, but those tables are all taken right now.

**妈妈**

How long is the wait?

**店员**

Probably fifteen or twenty minutes. I can't promise an exact time.

**弟弟 Ben**

My stomach says that is a very long time.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I look at the booth near the back. It has soft seats and a tiny picture of a dog wearing sunglasses above it. Actually, this table is fine.

**店员**

Right this way. Here are your menus. I'll give you a few minutes.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We slide into the booth. Ben immediately points at the dog picture and decides it's the owner of the restaurant.

**妈妈**

A dog who runs a diner? That's a busy dog.

**弟弟 Ben**

He probably checks all the hamburgers personally.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We order lunch and share a glass bottle of ketchup. By the time our food arrives, I have completely forgotten about the window. The booth is comfortable, Ben is telling dog stories, and Mom looks relaxed.

**妈妈**

Still wish we had waited?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Not at all. This table comes with entertainment. I point to Ben. Unfortunately, there doesn't seem to be an off switch.
