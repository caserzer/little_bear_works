# 35 · The Shoes-Off Question

做客时要脱鞋吗。第一次到Emma家做客，Lucy先问清楚家里的习惯。

情境表达：礼貌询问、表示感谢、适应主人习惯。

词语：guest — 客人；slippers — 拖鞋；comfortable — 舒服的。

常用句：

- Should I take my shoes off?
- Make yourself comfortable.
- Thanks for having me.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Shoes-Off Question. Emma invites me over after school. I've met her at the park and the library, but this is my first visit to her house. At the front door, I notice several pairs of shoes lined up neatly. Should I take my shoes off?

**朋友 Emma**

Yes, please. We usually leave them here. There are clean slippers in that basket if you'd like a pair.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thanks. I put my sneakers beside hers and choose a pair of slippers. They have little clouds on them, which makes walking feel slightly more interesting.

**朋友 Emma**

Make yourself comfortable. You can put your backpack by the chair.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Her room has a shelf full of drawings and a desk covered with colored pencils. I reach toward a tiny clay cat, then stop. May I pick this up?

**朋友 Emma**

Sure. Just hold the base. I made it in art class, and one ear is a little delicate.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I look at it carefully and put it back. We spend the afternoon drawing imaginary pets. Mine is a dog with enormous ears. Emma's is a very serious-looking purple turtle.

**朋友 Emma**

Want to stay for a snack? My mom has sliced some fruit.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

That sounds great. Before Dad picks me up, I put the pencils away and leave the slippers by the basket. Thanks for having me.

**朋友 Emma**

Come again soon. Your enormous-eared dog needs a friend.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Next time, I'll bring my sketchbook. The shoes can wait by the door while we invent another impossible animal.
