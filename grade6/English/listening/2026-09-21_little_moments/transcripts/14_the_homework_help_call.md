# 14 · The Homework Help Call

一起弄懂作业要求。Lucy和Emma通话核对作业，发现自己看错了页码。

情境表达：请求解释、澄清要求、核对信息。

词语：instructions — 说明；page — 页码；explain — 解释。

常用句：

- Could you explain that again?
- Which page are you on?
- That makes more sense.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Homework Help Call. I'm staring at my notebook when Emma calls. She's already finished our English assignment. I have written exactly one word, and that word is my name.

**朋友 Emma**

How's it going?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Slowly. I don't understand why we need to write about a jungle. Our class was talking about neighborhoods today.

**朋友 Emma**

A jungle? Which page are you on?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thirty-eight. The instructions say to describe an adventure. I was about to put a tiger next to the grocery store.

**朋友 Emma**

We're on page eighty-three. Describe your neighborhood, then add one place you'd like to have nearby.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I turn the pages. There it is. That makes more sense. Could you explain that again? Just the part about the new place.

**朋友 Emma**

Write about a real place first. Then imagine something useful or fun, like a swimming pool or a bigger park. The rest is your choice.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thanks. What did you pick?

**朋友 Emma**

A library with comfortable reading chairs. But you should choose something you actually want.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I look out my window at the empty lot down the street. I would love a little basketball court there. I start writing before the idea disappears.

**朋友 Emma**

All set now?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I think so. No more jungle.

**朋友 Emma**

You can save the tiger for another assignment.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I write the page number at the top of my notebook. Eighty-three. I check it twice. My neighborhood already feels much safer without that tiger beside the vegetables.
