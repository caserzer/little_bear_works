# 40 · The Best Part Was Tiny

最喜欢的都是小事。周日晚餐时，每个人分享一件最近让自己开心的小事。

情境表达：分享经历、追问细节、表达感受。

词语：favorite — 最喜欢的；moment — 时刻；notice — 留意。

常用句：

- What was your favorite part?
- That sounds like a good day.
- Mine was something small.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Best Part Was Tiny. At dinner on Sunday, Mom asks us a question. She wants to know one good thing from the week. Ben immediately says dessert, even though dessert hasn't happened yet.

**妈妈**

Something that already happened, please. Lucy, what was your favorite part?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Mine was something small. Emma saved me a seat at lunch on a day when I felt tired. She didn't make a big deal of it. She just moved her backpack and smiled.

**爸爸**

That sounds like a good day.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

It became a better day. What about you?

**爸爸**

I found the screwdriver I'd been looking for. It was exactly where I had put it away.

**弟弟 Ben**

Amazing. A mystery with no mystery.

**妈妈**

My favorite moment was hearing you two laugh while you folded the laundry. I almost didn't want to interrupt.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

That was the missing-sock investigation. A very serious operation. Ben thinks for a moment, then looks toward the kitchen window.

**弟弟 Ben**

Professor Leaf has a new leaf. I noticed it this morning.

**妈妈**

Really? You'll have to show us after dinner.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We all turn to look at the little plant in its yellow pot. Ben stands up proudly, as if he grew the leaf himself. I realize nobody chose anything expensive or enormous. A seat, a tool, a laugh, a leaf.

**爸爸**

Now, who would like dessert?

**弟弟 Ben**

Finally. My next favorite moment is arriving.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We pass the plates around. I decide I like this game. Next week, I'll pay a little more attention to the tiny good things, especially the ones that happen before dessert.
