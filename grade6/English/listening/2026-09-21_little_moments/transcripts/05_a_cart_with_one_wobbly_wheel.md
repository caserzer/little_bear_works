# 05 · A Cart With One Wobbly Wheel

购物车的小麻烦。Lucy和妈妈按清单购物，Ben一直试图加入新东西。

情境表达：寻找货架、核对清单、比较包装。

词语：cart — 购物车；aisle — 货架通道；list — 清单。

常用句：

- Which aisle is the cereal in?
- Is that on the list?
- We already have some at home.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. A Cart With One Wobbly Wheel. Our shopping cart makes a tiny squeak every time Mom turns a corner. Ben thinks it sounds like a mouse. I think it sounds like a mouse asking for a vacation.

**妈妈**

We need milk, eggs, bread, and cereal. Lucy, can you hold the list?

**弟弟 Ben**

And cookies. Very important cookies.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Is that on the list? Ben looks over my shoulder as if the word might appear if he stares hard enough. It doesn't. We reach the end of the aisle, but there's still no cereal.

**妈妈**

Excuse me. Which aisle is the cereal in?

**店员**

Aisle five, next to the oatmeal. The signs above you show the aisle numbers.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thanks. In aisle five, Ben picks up the biggest box he can reach. It's almost the size of his backpack.

**弟弟 Ben**

This one has a spaceship on it.

**妈妈**

Let's compare the smaller box, too. We only need enough for this week, and we already have some at home.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We choose the smaller box and cross cereal off the list. At checkout, I count four things. Milk, eggs, bread, and cereal. Perfect.

**弟弟 Ben**

The cart did all that work without getting any cookies.

**妈妈**

The cart doesn't eat cookies.

**弟弟 Ben**

That explains the squeaking. It's upset.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We park it with the other carts. I give it a little wave. Thanks for the ride, tiny mouse. Enjoy your break.
