# 28 · The Plant on the Windowsill

窗台上的小植物。一家人给新植物安排照顾计划，发现不能人人都额外浇一次水。

情境表达：询问是否完成、记录任务、商量照料。

词语：plant — 植物；soil — 土壤；care label — 养护标签。

常用句：

- Has anyone watered it today?
- Let's check the care label.
- I'll write it down.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Plant on the Windowsill. Mom brings home a small plant in a yellow pot. We put it by the kitchen window, and Ben immediately gives it a name: Professor Leaf. The professor is quiet but seems friendly.

**弟弟 Ben**

Has anyone watered it today?

**妈妈**

I watered it when we got home. Why?

**弟弟 Ben**

I was going to give it a welcome drink.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I was thinking the same thing. Mom moves the watering can back to the shelf before Professor Leaf gets three welcomes in one afternoon.

**妈妈**

Let's check the care label. Different plants need different amounts of water. This one doesn't need a drink every time someone says hello.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We read the little label together. Mom shows us how she checks the soil and where to put a saucer under the pot.

**弟弟 Ben**

We need a schedule. Important professors have schedules.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I'll write it down. I make a small card where we can record when the plant was watered. We also agree to ask Mom before adding any more.

**妈妈**

Good. You can both help, as long as you check first.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The next morning, Ben greets the professor on his way to breakfast. He looks at the card, puts down the watering can, and gives the plant a tiny bow instead.

**弟弟 Ben**

No drink today, Professor. Just respect.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The leaves don't move. I think that means the professor approves. Or maybe he's busy preparing a lecture about why people should stop poking his soil.
