# 32 · The Return With a Receipt

带着小票去退换。买回来的笔记本有印刷问题，Lucy和爸爸到店里说明情况。

情境表达：描述商品问题、提出换货、出示凭证。

词语：receipt — 小票；exchange — 换货；blank — 空白的。

常用句：

- I'd like to exchange this.
- Do you have the receipt?
- The pages are printed upside down.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Return With a Receipt. I buy a new notebook with tiny stars on the cover. At home, I open it and discover that half the pages have the date and title boxes printed upside down. Unless I plan to write while standing on my head, something is wrong.

**爸爸**

Looks like a printing problem. We can take it back and ask about an exchange.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The next day, we return to the store. I keep the notebook and receipt together in a small bag. At the counter, I take a breath. I'd like to exchange this.

**店员**

What's the problem with it?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The pages are printed upside down. I open the notebook and show her where the pattern changes.

**店员**

Oh, I see. Do you have the receipt?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes, right here.

**店员**

Thanks. We can exchange it for another one. Would you like the same design?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Definitely. I still like the stars. The clerk brings another notebook, and we check it together before I put it in my bag. All the pages look right this time.

**爸爸**

Nicely handled. You explained the problem clearly.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I was worried I would sound rude.

**爸爸**

Asking politely about a problem is okay. That's part of what the service desk is there for.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

At home, I write my name on the first page. The stars are still excellent, and my words are the right way up. Ben seems disappointed. He was looking forward to the upside-down writing experiment.
