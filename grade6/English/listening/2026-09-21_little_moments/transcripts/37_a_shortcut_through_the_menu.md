# 37 · A Shortcut Through the Menu

看懂陌生的菜单。Lucy不认识菜单上的一道菜，直接向服务员问清楚。

情境表达：询问食材、说明偏好、请求分开放。

词语：ingredients — 食材；on the side — 另外分开放；spicy — 辣的。

常用句：

- What's in this sandwich?
- Is the sauce spicy?
- Could I have the sauce on the side?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. A Shortcut Through the Menu. Mom and I try a new sandwich shop. The menu has names like Garden Stack and Sunset Special. They sound beautiful, but I have no idea what I'm supposed to eat.

**妈妈**

You can ask. A name doesn't tell you everything.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I point to the Garden Stack when the server comes over. What's in this sandwich?

**店员**

Roasted vegetables, cheese, lettuce, and our house sauce, on toasted bread.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Is the sauce spicy?

**店员**

A little. It has some chili in it. If you prefer, we can put it in a separate little cup.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Could I have the sauce on the side?

**店员**

Absolutely. Anything else you'd like to know?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

No, thank you. That sounds good. Mom orders a different sandwich, and we sit by a shelf of plants. When our food arrives, I try a tiny bit of sauce first.

**妈妈**

How is it?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Good, but a little is enough for me. I add some to one corner of the sandwich instead of pouring it over everything.

**妈妈**

Glad you asked?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Very glad. I would have guessed Garden Stack meant a pile of lettuce with a flower on top.

**店员**

Everything okay here?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes, thanks. Your garden is delicious. The server laughs, and Mom smiles over her sandwich. Next time a menu sounds like poetry, I'll ask about the ingredients. It's easier than trying to eat a sunset.
