# 03 · The Lunchbox Mix-Up

拿错的午餐盒。午饭时间打开盒子，Lucy发现自己拿了爸爸的午餐。

情境表达：说明拿错、分享食物、提出替代办法。

词语：lunchbox — 午餐盒；mix-up — 弄混；trade — 交换。

常用句：

- I think I grabbed the wrong one.
- Would you like half?
- Let's check the labels tomorrow.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Lunchbox Mix-Up. At lunch, Emma sits across from me and opens her sandwich. I open my blue lunchbox, expecting pasta. Inside, I find a giant salad, a small jar of dressing, and a note that says, Have a great meeting.

**朋友 Emma**

Wow. Is your sandwich hiding under all that lettuce?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I think I grabbed the wrong one. Dad and I have exactly the same lunchbox. Mine was supposed to have pasta and apple slices.

**朋友 Emma**

So your dad is going to a meeting with apple slices and a dinosaur fork?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I picture him opening the box. Actually, he probably likes the dinosaur fork. I send Mom a message through the school office, just so nobody worries.

**朋友 Emma**

I have an extra bread roll. Would you like half?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thanks. I have plenty of cucumber if you want some. We check that the food is okay for both of us, then put a clean napkin between our lunches.

**朋友 Emma**

Look at us. We accidentally opened a tiny restaurant.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

That afternoon, Dad is waiting at home with both empty lunchboxes. He holds up the dinosaur fork like a prize.

**爸爸**

Excellent pasta. Very professional fork. How was my salad?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Crunchy. Really crunchy. Let's check the labels tomorrow.

**爸爸**

Good idea. Mine can say Dad, and yours can say Lucy.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Ben suggests a third label for himself: Person Who Would Like More Pasta. We give him a sticker, but we keep our lunchboxes.
