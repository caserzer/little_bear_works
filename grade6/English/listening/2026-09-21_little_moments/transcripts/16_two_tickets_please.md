# 16 · Two Tickets, Please

买两张电影票。选座、买票和找影厅，Lucy第一次认真参与安排。

情境表达：选择座位、确认时间、询问位置。

词语：ticket — 票；row — 排；middle — 中间。

常用句：

- Two tickets, please.
- Are there seats near the middle?
- Which way is theater three?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. Two Tickets, Please. Dad and I arrive at the movie theater twenty minutes early. I have one job today: help choose the seats. Dad says this is more important than choosing the popcorn. I am not completely convinced.

**店员**

Hi there. What would you like to see?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

The animated movie at two thirty. Two tickets, please.

**店员**

Sure. Here's the seating chart. The gray seats are taken, and the blue ones are available.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Are there seats near the middle?

**店员**

Row six has two together. Or you can sit closer to the front in row three.

**爸爸**

Row six sounds good to me. What do you think?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Perfect. I don't want to spend the whole movie looking straight up. We get our tickets and move away from the counter. Then I realize I forgot to ask one thing. Which way is theater three?

**店员**

Down the hall on your left, just past the restrooms.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thanks. We find our seats with plenty of time. I check the numbers twice before we sit down.

**爸爸**

Excellent planning. Now, about that popcorn.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I knew it was important. We share a small bag and watch the screen fill with trailers. Before the movie starts, Dad silences his phone, and I do the same.

**爸爸**

Ready for the adventure?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes. Middle seats, snacks, and no neck pain. I think my first day as the family seat expert is going pretty well.
