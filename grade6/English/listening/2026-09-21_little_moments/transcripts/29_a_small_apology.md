# 29 · A Small Apology

一句认真说的对不起。Lucy不小心弄坏了朋友的书角，决定直接说明并想办法补救。

情境表达：承认错误、道歉、提出补救。

词语：accident — 意外；cover — 封面；repair — 修补。

常用句：

- I'm really sorry.
- I should have been more careful.
- What can I do to help?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. A Small Apology. Emma lends me her favorite comic book. I put it on my desk, right beside a cup of water. You can probably guess what happens next. My elbow moves, the cup tips, and the corner of the cover gets wet.

**弟弟 Ben**

Oh. That doesn't look good.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

No, it doesn't. I move the cup, gently dry the book, and leave it open where it won't get knocked again. The corner still looks bent. For a minute, I wish I could pretend it was always like that.

**弟弟 Ben**

Are you going to tell Emma?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes. I just need to stop being nervous first. The next day, I give her the book and explain what happened. I'm really sorry. I should have been more careful.

**朋友 Emma**

Thanks for telling me. I am disappointed, though. I really like this book.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I know. What can I do to help? I can replace it with my allowance if you'd like.

**朋友 Emma**

Let me see how it looks when it's fully dry. Maybe we can flatten the corner. I'll tell you if I want a replacement.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Okay. I won't try to fix anything else without asking. We sit together at lunch. It feels a little awkward at first, but then Emma starts talking about the next book in the series. At home that afternoon, Ben wants an update.

**弟弟 Ben**

Are you still friends?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Yes. Friends can be disappointed and still be friends. Also, all future cups of water are getting their own table. Far away from the comic books.
