# 33 · The After-School Club Choice

选哪个课后社团。Lucy和Emma想参加不同的社团，决定分别尝试再交流。

情境表达：询问活动、表达兴趣、尊重不同选择。

词语：club — 社团；sign up — 报名；try out — 尝试。

常用句：

- What happens at the first meeting?
- Do I need any experience?
- We don't have to choose the same one.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The After-School Club Choice. The school hallway is covered with club posters. Emma stops in front of the art club sign. I stop in front of the cooking club sign. There's a picture of a very attractive muffin on it.

**朋友 Emma**

You're choosing based on the picture, aren't you?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Partly. But I also want to learn how to make something besides toast. We walk over to the teacher at the cooking club table. What happens at the first meeting?

**烹饪社老师**

We'll tour the kitchen, talk about the rules, and plan a simple snack together.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Do I need any experience?

**烹饪社老师**

No. Beginners are welcome. Bring your curiosity, and we'll start with the basics.

**朋友 Emma**

It sounds fun. But I still really want to try art club.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We don't have to choose the same one. You can show me what you make, and I can tell you what I cook.

**朋友 Emma**

Can you also bring me what you cook?

**烹饪社老师**

That depends on the recipe and our sharing rules. But you'll definitely have stories to exchange.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We check the meeting days and take the sign-up information home for our parents. Emma points out that both clubs meet on Wednesdays.

**朋友 Emma**

We can still walk home together afterward.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Perfect. You'll have paint on your hands, and I'll probably have flour on my sleeves. Different clubs, same walk home. I look once more at the muffin picture. I hope my curiosity is ready, because my appetite certainly is.
