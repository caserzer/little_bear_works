# 09 · The Bus Going the Other Way

公交车方向坐反了？。Lucy和爸爸在上车前发现站错方向，及时换到马路对面。

情境表达：问方向、确认站点、询问车程。

词语：across the street — 在街道对面；stop — 站点；direction — 方向。

常用句：

- Does this bus go to the museum?
- You're on the wrong side of the street.
- How many stops is it?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Bus Going the Other Way. Dad and I are taking the bus to the museum. We find a stop with the right number and wait under the sign. I feel very proud of my excellent planning.

**爸爸**

Did you check which direction it's going?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Same number, same bus. Isn't that enough? Dad points across the street. There's another stop with the same number. Before I can answer, a bus pulls up.

**去程方向司机**

Morning. Where are you headed?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Does this bus go to the museum?

**去程方向司机**

It does on the way back, but you're on the wrong side of the street. You want the stop across from this one.

**爸爸**

Thanks for checking. We'll cross at the lights.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We wait for the crossing signal and walk to the other stop. The little route map shows the museum four stops away. My excellent planning needs a small update.

**爸爸**

Good thing we asked before we sat down.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

When the next bus arrives, I ask the driver again. This time, she says yes. How many stops is it?

**返程方向司机**

Four from here. I'll call out the museum stop, too.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Thank you. We sit near the middle, and I watch the names appear on the screen.

**爸爸**

Still our chief trip planner?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Absolutely. I've just added a new rule to my system. Check the number, check the direction, and don't act like a genius until you're on the right bus.
