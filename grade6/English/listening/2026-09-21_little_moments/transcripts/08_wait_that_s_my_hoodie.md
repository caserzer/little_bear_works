# 08 · Wait, That's My Hoodie

等等，那是我的卫衣。Lucy想穿的卫衣不见了，原来Ben把它当成了自己的新衣服。

情境表达：描述衣物、请求借用、约定归还。

词语：hoodie — 连帽卫衣；sleeve — 袖子；borrow — 借用。

常用句：

- Can I borrow it?
- It looks a little big on you.
- Please ask me first next time.

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. Wait, That's My Hoodie. I'm getting ready to meet Emma when I notice something missing. My favorite green hoodie isn't on my chair. It isn't in my closet, either. Then a small green shape walks past my door.

**弟弟 Ben**

Morning. Nice weather, right?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Wait, that's my hoodie. Ben stops. The sleeves cover both his hands, and the bottom reaches almost to his knees. It looks a little big on you.

**弟弟 Ben**

That's the style. Very comfortable. Lots of room for snacks.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

I was going to wear it today. Did you take it from my chair?

**弟弟 Ben**

Maybe. Can I borrow it?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

You're supposed to ask that before you put it on.

**妈妈**

Everything okay in here?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

We're having a small fashion discussion. I need my hoodie, and Ben seems to be living inside it.

**弟弟 Ben**

I like the big pocket. My own sweatshirt doesn't have one.

**妈妈**

Your blue jacket has two pockets. It's hanging by the door.

**弟弟 Ben**

Two pockets? That does change things.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

He pulls off the hoodie and hands it back. I check the pocket and find a toy car, a clean tissue, and a tiny rubber duck. Please ask me first next time.

**弟弟 Ben**

Deal. Can I borrow it tomorrow?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Maybe. Ask me tomorrow. For now, your duck needs a new apartment.

**弟弟 Ben**

The blue jacket has room. He can have his own pocket.
