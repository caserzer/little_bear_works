# 22 · The Video Call With Grandma

给奶奶打视频电话。一家人视频聊天，先解决镜头角度和听不清的小问题。

情境表达：检查声音、调整镜头、分享近况。

词语：camera — 摄像头；volume — 音量；hear — 听见。

常用句：

- Can you hear us now?
- Move the camera a little higher.
- What have you been up to?

## 分角色英文原文

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Little Moments. The Video Call With Grandma. Every Sunday, Mom calls Grandma, and we all squeeze onto the couch to say hello. Today, Grandma can see the ceiling, the top of Dad's head, and absolutely none of us.

**妈妈**

Move the camera a little higher. No, the other way. There. Now I can see everybody.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Mom is talking to Dad, who is balancing the tablet against a pillow. Grandma waves on the screen, but we can't hear her yet.

**爸爸**

I turned the volume down earlier. Let me fix that.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Can you hear us now? Grandma gives us a thumbs-up. She says she can hear Ben better than anyone, which is not surprising. He's sitting closest to the microphone.

**弟弟 Ben**

Hi, Grandma! I lost a tooth!

**妈妈**

You don't have to shout. She's far away, but the microphone is right here.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Grandma laughs and asks us what we've been doing. Mom points the camera toward me. What have you been up to? That's Grandma's favorite question, and this time I have something ready to show her.

**弟弟 Ben**

Lucy made a poster. I helped by not spilling anything on it.

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

A very valuable contribution. I hold up the poster and tell Grandma about our neighborhood project. Then Ben shows the gap in his teeth from three different angles.

**爸爸**

Same time next Sunday?

**Lucy／第一人称旁白**

Grandma nods. We wave goodbye, and Dad carefully moves the tablet off the pillow. Next week, we'll try to show her our faces right from the beginning.
