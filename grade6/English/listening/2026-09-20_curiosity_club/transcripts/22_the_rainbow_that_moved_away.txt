Ms. Park explains that a rainbow is not a solid object resting on the ground. It is an effect of light reaching their eyes from water droplets.

Sunlight contains different colors. As light enters and leaves droplets, it changes direction. Reflection inside the drops also helps send some light back toward an observer.
