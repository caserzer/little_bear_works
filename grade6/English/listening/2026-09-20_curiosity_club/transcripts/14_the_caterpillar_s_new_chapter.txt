Now, a little more to discover.

A butterfly's life cycle includes egg, larva, pupa, and adult. The caterpillar is the larva, and the chrysalis is the pupal stage. Metamorphosis changes the insect's body form. A quiet-looking chrysalis is part of an active biological process.
